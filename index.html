Joanna is in 8th grade. She builds robots, and she plays golf seriously. Her current robot already hunts for a golf ball, and she has started a girls' robotics club called SheBots. She is weighing biology, medicine and biomedical engineering, with engineering pulling hardest today.Joanna 现在读 8 年级。她做机器人，也认真打高尔夫，而且打得不错。她正在做的机器人，目标物就是一颗高尔夫球；她还发起了一个女生机器人社团 SheBots。她在生物、医学和生物医学工程之间考虑，眼下最吸引她的是工程和机器人。

The spine主线 Joanna builds machines that measure how a body moves: first a golfer's swing, then a patient's recovery, and finally kits that girls she trained build and give to people who need them. Joanna 做的是“测量身体如何运动”的机器：先测高尔夫挥杆，再测病人的康复，最后由她带出来的女生们亲手组装这些工具，送到需要的人手里。

How we read the brief. The plan runs grade 8 (2026–27) to grade 12 (2030–31). We aimed for biomedical engineering as the lead major, because it keeps her robotics strength and opens toward medicine; biology and pre-med remain supported. The non-profit is in place by 11th grade, as asked. Grade 8 · 2026–27 · Foundation8 年级 · 2026–27 · 打基础Under way进行中

The Putting Robot推杆机器人

A small robot that sees a golf ball, drives to it, picks it up, sets it down, and putts it at a target, then reports how often it gets close.一台小机器人：看见高尔夫球，开过去，抓起来，放好，再推杆打向目标，最后统计它打得有多准。

This year builds on Joanna's existing proposal, Step into Robotics (November 2025). Nothing in it is replaced. We keep every step and add three things at the end that turn a working demo into the first piece of evidence in her story.这一年建立在 Joanna 现有的方案《Step into Robotics》（2025 年 11 月）之上，原有内容一项不删。我们保留每一步，只在最后加三件事，把一个能跑的演示变成她故事里的第一份证据。

Already in the proposal原方案已有的内容

  • Existing已有Python and Arduino: an L298N-driven two-wheel car.Python 与 Arduino：L298N 驱动的双轮小车。
  • Existing已有Design and printing: TinkerCAD parts, sliced in Bambu Studio, printed on the H2D.建模与打印：TinkerCAD 建模，Bambu Studio 切片，H2D 打印底盘和支架。
  • Existing已有Simulation: her own STL files in Isaac Sim, with the car simulated to "see ball, move closer".仿真：把自己的 STL 导入 Isaac Sim，仿真小车“看到球、靠近球”。
  • Existing已有Vision and grasping: OpenCV golf-ball detection, a printed servo gripper, the loop Recognize → Approach → Grasp → Place.视觉与抓取：OpenCV 识别高尔夫球，打印舵机抓手，完成“识别 → 接近 → 抓取 → 放置”闭环。
  • Existing已有Final goal: hit the ball toward a target. Demo video and report.最终目标：把球击向目标，完成演示视频和项目报告。

What we add我们新增的内容

  • New新增A putting arm with a sensor on it. The gripper hands off to a printed, servo-driven putter arm. An IMU (a motion sensor, the BNO085) rides on the arm and logs its speed at impact. This is her first motion sensor. It is the same part she will strap to a golfer next year.带传感器的推杆臂。抓手放好球后，交给一支 3D 打印、舵机驱动的推杆臂。推杆臂上装一个 IMU（运动传感器，型号 BNO085），记录击球瞬间的速度。这是她第一次用运动传感器，明年绑在球员身上的正是同一个零件。
  • New新增An experiment instead of a demo. 50 putts each at 1, 2 and 3 metres, first in Isaac Sim and then on the real robot. She measures how far each ball stops from the hole and asks one question: how much accuracy is lost between simulation and the real world, and why? That is a science fair project. A video of one good putt is not.把演示变成实验。在 1 米、2 米、3 米三个距离各推 50 杆，先在 Isaac Sim 里做，再在实物上做。记录每颗球停在离洞多远，回答一个问题：从仿真到现实，精度损失了多少，原因是什么？这才是一个科学展项目；一段推进一杆的视频不是。
  • New新增SheBots gets a curriculum. Joanna turns the first half of her own pathway (car, printing, ball detection) into six club sessions. The club needs something to build, and teaching it is the fastest way for her to find what she half-understands.SheBots 有了课程。Joanna 把自己学习路径的前半段（小车、打印、识别球）整理成 6 次社团活动。社团需要一个实际要做的东西，而去教别人，也是她发现自己哪里没真正弄懂的最快办法。
Why it comes now. She is already halfway there. What this year adds is the habit that the rest of the plan depends on: measure it, repeat it, compare it. And it leaves her with a machine that can make the same stroke 150 times. Next year, that machine becomes the reference she tests human-worn sensors against.为什么是现在。她已经走了一半。这一年真正加上的，是后面所有项目都依赖的习惯：测量、重复、对比。同时她会留下一台能把同一杆重复 150 次的机器。明年，这台机器就是检验人体佩戴传感器准不准的“标尺”。

Putter arm: one high-torque servo (MG996R class) swinging a printed arm with a short aluminium or printed face; the swing angle sets ball speed. BNO085 on I²C to the Arduino or a Raspberry Pi, logging angular velocity at 100 Hz. Ball distance from hole measured from an overhead phone video, one frame per putt, using the same OpenCV ball detector. Grade 9 · 2027–28 · Research9 年级 · 2027–28 · 科研

SwingSenseSwingSense 挥杆传感

Can three cheap motion sensors and a phone camera measure the parts of a golf swing that are linked to back and wrist injury, accurately enough to be useful?三个便宜的运动传感器加一部手机，能不能把挥杆中与腰伤、腕伤相关的动作测得足够准，准到真正有用？

Low back pain is the most commonly reported injury among golfers in published surveys, and the lead wrist is close behind. Coaches and researchers measure swings with motion-capture labs that cost tens of thousands of dollars. Joanna asks whether a $60 kit can get close.在已发表的调查中，下背痛是高尔夫球员最常报告的伤病，前导手手腕紧随其后。教练和研究者通常在动作捕捉实验室里分析挥杆，设备动辄数万美元。Joanna 要回答的是：一套 60 美元的设备能接近到什么程度。

She builds a set of three wireless sensors (lead wrist, chest, pelvis) and pairs them with phone video run through MediaPipe Pose. She measures three things: how far the shoulders turn past the hips (the "X-factor"), how fast the trunk rotates, and how far the lead wrist bends. Then she checks the sensors two ways. First against her putting robot, whose arm angle she knows exactly. Then against slow-motion video of real swings from herself and teammates.她做一套三个无线传感器（前导手腕、胸口、骨盆），配合手机视频和 MediaPipe Pose 姿态识别。测三个量：肩部比髋部多转了多少（所谓 “X-factor”）、躯干旋转多快、前导手腕弯曲多大。然后用两种方式校验：先对照推杆机器人，它的摆臂角度是精确已知的；再对照她本人和队友真实挥杆的慢动作视频。

Why it comes now. Without last year's robot she has no ground truth, and without ground truth this is a gadget, not a study. The IMU she learned on the putter arm is the same chip. This year also moves her from machines to bodies, the first step toward biomedical work. And it produces the measurement pipeline that next year's rehab device runs on.为什么是现在。没有去年的机器人，她就没有“标准答案”；没有标准答案，这只是个小玩意，算不上研究。她在推杆臂上用过的 IMU 就是同一颗芯片。这一年也让她从“测机器”走到“测人体”，迈出生物医学方向的第一步。它留下的测量流程，正是明年康复设备要用的。
She learns学到的技能
ESP32 and Bluetooth, sensor fusion and calibration, MediaPipe pose estimation, Python data analysis (pandas, matplotlib), Bland–Altman agreement analysis, how to run a study on people ethically, basic biomechanics.ESP32 与蓝牙、传感器融合与校准、MediaPipe 姿态估计、Python 数据分析（pandas、matplotlib）、Bland–Altman 一致性分析、如何合乎伦理地开展人体研究、基础生物力学。
Deliverables成果
Three-sensor kit with printed housings; open-source code on GitHub; a dataset of roughly 200 swings from 10 to 12 golfers; a written research paper; a fair board.带 3D 打印外壳的三传感器套件；GitHub 开源代码；来自 10–12 名球员、约 200 次挥杆的数据集；一篇研究论文；科学展展板。
Outside validation外部认可
Regional fair in early spring, then the state fair if she qualifies (in California, the California Science & Engineering Fair, usually in April). Qualifying for Regeneron ISEF is possible but should not be expected in 9th grade. The brief names Dr. Tang's robotics lab at Cal Poly Pomona; we have not been able to confirm it. If it is real and open to younger students, this paper is what she would bring to ask for a summer place.初春参加地区科学展，晋级后参加州赛（加州为 California Science & Engineering Fair，通常在 4 月）。晋级 Regeneron ISEF 有可能，但 9 年级不应抱这个预期。需求中提到 Cal Poly Pomona 的 Dr. Tang 机器人实验室，我们无法核实；如果确有此实验室且接收低年级学生，这篇论文就是她申请暑期机会的敲门砖。
How hard难度
6 to 8 hours a week, more during data collection. The hard part is the human-subjects paperwork: science fairs require approval from a review board before any data is collected from people, including teammates. Start that in September. Second risk: wrist sensors drift during a fast swing. If wrist data stays unreliable, she drops the wrist and reports the trunk alone.每周 6–8 小时，采集数据期间更多。难点是人体研究的审批：科学展要求在采集任何人的数据之前（包括队友）先获得审查委员会批准，9 月就要开始办。第二个风险是快速挥杆时腕部传感器漂移；如果腕部数据始终不可靠，就放弃腕部，只报告躯干结果。

Grade 10 · 2028–29 · Community10 年级 · 2028–29 · 社区

ReMotion WristReMotion 腕部康复训练器

A 3D-printed handle with a motion sensor inside, and a tablet app that turns daily wrist exercises into a game and records range of motion so a therapist can see progress between visits.一个内置运动传感器的 3D 打印手柄，配一个平板 App：把每天的手腕康复练习变成小游戏，并记录关节活动度，让治疗师在两次复诊之间也能看到进展。

This is the year the story turns from golf to medicine. A broken wrist is one of the most common fractures, and recovery depends on months of repetitive exercises at home that many people stop doing. Joanna already knows how to measure a wrist from SwingSense. Now the question is who that helps.这一年，故事从高尔夫转向医学。腕部骨折是最常见的骨折之一，康复要靠回家后几个月重复枯燥的练习，很多人练着练着就放弃了。Joanna 在 SwingSense 中已经学会怎样测量手腕；现在的问题是：这能帮到谁。

She builds it with a team of three or four SheBots members, who enter it together in Technovation Girls, a real programme where girls build an app and a business plan. She asks a local physical therapist to advise. The therapist does not need to be a researcher; a clinic willing to give feedback is enough. Accuracy is tested on healthy volunteers against a standard goniometer. Patients use it only if a clinician chooses to try it, under their care.她和三四名 SheBots 成员组队来做，并以团队名义参加 Technovation Girls，这是一个真实存在的项目，女生团队要做出 App 和商业计划。她会请一位本地物理治疗师担任顾问，对方不必是研究者，愿意给反馈的诊所就够了。精度在健康志愿者身上测试，以标准量角器作对照；只有临床医生自己决定试用时，病人才会在其指导下使用。

Why it comes now. The sensors, the calibration method and the analysis code all come from SwingSense; without them this would be a two-year project. SheBots has run for two years by now, so there are members ready to build something real. And this year produces what the non-profit will need on its first day: a device, a design that girls can assemble, and a clinician who knows her.为什么是现在。传感器、校准方法和分析代码全部来自 SwingSense，没有它们这就得是个两年的项目。到这时 SheBots 已经运行两年，有成员能做真东西了。这一年还为非营利组织准备好开张第一天需要的一切：一件设备、一套女生们能组装的设计，以及一位认识她的临床人士。

The capstone: SheBots Motion Lab收官之作：SheBots Motion Lab

What it is. A girl-led non-profit with two halves that feed each other. Girls aged 11 to 16 learn robotics by building ReMotion wrist trainers in club sessions. The finished trainers are lent, free, through partner physical therapy clinics to patients recovering at home, and returned for the next person.它是什么。一个由女生主导的非营利组织，两部分相互支撑：11–16 岁的女生在社团活动中通过组装 ReMotion 腕部训练器学习机器人；做好的训练器经合作的物理治疗诊所，免费借给在家康复的病人，用完归还，再借给下一位。

Who it serves. Two groups: girls who would not otherwise touch a soldering iron, and patients whose recovery happens mostly at home, alone.服务谁。两类人：原本不会接触电烙铁的女生，以及大部分康复时间都独自在家的病人。

What it needs to be real要成为真正的组织，还需要什么

A fiscal home, not a 501(c)(3) at first. A 16-year-old filing for tax-exempt status is slow and, in most states, needs adult directors. A fiscal sponsor lets her accept donations legally from week one. Hack Club's fiscal sponsorship for teen-led projects is one option to check; a local community foundation is another. Formal 501(c)(3) registration can follow if the organisation is still growing in grade 12.先找财务托管，而不是一开始就注册 501(c)(3)。16 岁的学生申请免税资格既慢，而且在大多数州需要成年人担任董事。通过财务托管方，她从第一周起就能合法接受捐款。Hack Club 为青少年项目提供的财务托管是可以核实的一个选择，本地社区基金会是另一个。如果到 12 年级组织仍在发展，再正式注册 501(c)(3)。

Partners. Two physical therapy clinics that lend the trainers; one school or library that hosts a second SheBots chapter. Advisors. A clinician, an engineer (the lab mentor, if that placement happens), and a parent or teacher who handles anything a minor cannot sign. Targets for the year: two chapters, 25 girls trained, 20 trainers built, 30 loans completed. These are goals, not forecasts.合作方。两家负责出借训练器的物理治疗诊所；一所学校或图书馆承办 SheBots 第二分队。顾问。一位临床人士、一位工程师（如果实验室实习成行，可由导师担任），以及一位负责未成年人不能签署事项的家长或老师。年度目标：两个分队、培训 25 名女生、组装 20 台训练器、完成 30 次出借。这些是目标，不是预测。

What an admissions reader sees招生官会看到什么

The story in one sentence: a golfer who built a robot to putt, used it to test sensors that measure swings, turned that into a rehab tool, and then taught other girls to build it for patients.一句话的故事：一个打高尔夫的女生，先造了会推杆的机器人，再用它校验测量挥杆的传感器，又把传感器做成康复工具，最后教其他女生为病人组装它。

Strongest evidence. The SwingSense paper, because it shows she can ask a question and check her own answer. Then the handover in grade 12, because it shows the organisation did not depend on her alone.最有力的证据。SwingSense 论文，因为它证明她能提出问题并检验自己的答案；其次是 12 年级的交接，它证明组织不靠她一个人撑着。

Majors it supports. Biomedical engineering most directly; mechanical engineering and robotics; kinesiology. Pre-med is supported through the clinical partnership.支撑的专业。最直接的是生物医学工程；其次是机械工程与机器人、运动机能学。通过与临床的合作，医学预科方向也站得住。

What it does not prove. Any wet-lab biology: there is no cell, gene or tissue work here. If she moves toward pure biology, she needs a biology course or summer programme to show it. It also does not prove clinical benefit. The trainer is tested for accuracy and usability, not for whether patients recover faster, and the page should never claim otherwise.它证明不了什么。湿实验生物学：这里没有细胞、基因或组织层面的工作。如果她转向纯生物方向，需要用生物课程或暑期项目来补上。它也证明不了临床疗效：训练器测的是精度和易用性，而不是病人是否恢复得更快，任何材料都不应这样宣称。
